Investment thesis

  • A multiple bet, not a franchise bet. The P/E multiple explains 84% of Monte Carlo variance. AAPL-SVC-X AAPL-TERM
  • Services carries the premium. Services must keep compounding at a double-digit pace for the multiple to hold. AAPL-SVC-G
  • Regulatory rents fund it. The ~$20bn+ Google default payment and App Store take are under active antitrust and DMA attack. AAPL-GOOG
  • The AI upgrade cycle is optionality, not base case. Apple Intelligence earns no direct revenue today. AAPL-IPH-G

Design notes — what the new validation gates find on today's page

  1. Equity bridge sign. The DCF adds '+ net cash $55.0bn' to reach equity, but the balance sheet and inputs table report net debt of $57.7bn. The difference is about $113bn, roughly $7.5 a share on 15.0bn diluted shares (the SOTP lever row also adds net cash). Period/sign coherence check (AF-031) blocks the value until resolved.
  2. Guidance label (D-372). 'FY+1 guided revenue $465.0bn' and 'FY+1 guided EPS $8.5' are typed 'company guidance'. Apple does not issue annual revenue or EPS guidance; these are house estimates.
  3. Model coherence. Monte Carlo puts 7% of paths above spot while the authored scenarios put 30% of probability above spot. With PWEV and MC on shared drivers, this becomes a validation item to resolve, not a footnote.
  4. Caption mismatch. The scenario chart caption reads 'Five-scenario tree'; four scenarios are authored (the cross-check table says so).
  5. Missing rationale. All five thesis-break triggers read 'rationale withheld pending re-authoring'. The foundation schema makes a rationale mandatory for every invalidation condition.
  6. Stale description. The company overview quotes 2020 revenue ($274.5bn) from the vendor description. C19 refreshes the overview from the 10-K Item 1 digest.

These come straight from the live AAPL page. In the new pipeline the first three block or flag the affected value before publication; the last three are caught by schema and freshness rules.
